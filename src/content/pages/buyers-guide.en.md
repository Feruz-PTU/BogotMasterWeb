This guide is for the people who buy licorice root for a company: procurement and quality staff at pharmaceutical, extraction, food, cosmetics and tobacco companies. Each chapter covers one part of the job: choosing a form and grade, reading a specification and a certificate of analysis, the shipping documents, placing an order, the customs codes and import rules, and the trade terms explained in a glossary.

For shipping terms and documents, see [Shipping, Incoterms and documents](/logistics).
